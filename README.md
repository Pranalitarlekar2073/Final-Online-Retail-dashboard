# 📊 Online Retail Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Online Retail Sales Dashboard** is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** created to analyze retail sales data and generate meaningful business insights.

The dashboard provides a clear view of **revenue, invoices, customer details, country-wise sales, product quantities, and monthly revenue trends**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall retail revenue
* Track the number of invoices
* Understand revenue contribution by country
* Analyze product-wise quantity sold
* Identify monthly revenue trends
* Explore customer and transaction details
* Identify factors influencing Total Price using Power BI AI Insights

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI Desktop**
* **Power BI Data Visualization**
* **Power BI AI Insights**
* **Interactive Filters & Slicers**
* **Data Analysis & Visualization**

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

### 💰 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard displays:

* **Total Revenue:** 8.79M
* **Invoice Count:** 392.839K

The dashboard also provides revenue share information by country.

### 🌍 Revenue Share by Country

The dashboard analyzes revenue generated from different countries, including:

* United Kingdom
* Netherlands
* EIRE
* Germany
* France
* Australia
* Austria
* Bahrain
* Belgium
* Brazil

The **United Kingdom** contributes the largest share shown in the dashboard.

### 📦 Quantity Sold by Product

The dashboard visualizes the quantity sold for different products, including products such as:

* PAPER ...
* MEDIUM ...
* WORLD ...
* JUMBO ...
* WHITE ...
* ASSORTED ...
* PACK ...
* POPCORN ...
* RABBIT ...
* MINI ...

This helps identify products with higher sales quantities.

### 📅 Monthly Revenue Trend

A monthly revenue chart is included to analyze revenue patterns from:

**January → December**

This helps understand changes in revenue throughout the year.

### 👥 Customer & Transaction Details

The dashboard contains detailed transaction-level information such as:

* Invoice Number
* Customer ID
* Product Description
* Total Price
* Country

The report includes customer and transaction details across multiple countries.

### 🤖 AI Insights

Power BI's **Key Influencers** visual is used to identify factors affecting Total Price.

One highlighted insight is that **Country = Netherlands** is associated with a higher average TotalPrice in the displayed analysis.
